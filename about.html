Who we are

Modulabz Interiors

Modulabz Interiors (Vraj Interiors) is an interior design and execution company based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. We focus on turnkey interior design and complete project execution for homes, villas, offices and shops.

We believe getting your interiors done should be simple. That is why one team handles everything — understanding your needs, preparing 2D and 3D designs, and finishing the work on site — so you have one point of contact from the first meeting to the final handover.

  • Residential & commercial interiors
  • 2D layouts & 3D designs
  • Expert site supervision
  • Complete turnkey execution
  • Clear, itemised quotations
  • Projects in 4 cities

Our approach

Our Mission & Vision

Mission — to create interiors that are beautiful, practical and built to last, delivered on time and within the agreed budget.

Vision — to be the most trusted interior company for families and businesses in Gujarat and beyond, known for honest pricing and quality work.

How we work

Our Process

A simple, step-by-step process so you always know what happens next.

Consultation

We meet, visit your site, take measurements and understand your needs and budget.

2D Layout

We plan furniture and space in 2D — with Vastu if you need it.

3D Design & Quote

You see your rooms in 3D and receive an itemised quotation.

Execution

Our team completes the work on site, with regular updates to you.

Handover

Final checks, cleaning and handover of your finished space.
